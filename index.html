Drafts

Stories, notes, and works in progress.

The rain had been falling since morning, soft enough to ignore but persistent enough to notice. From the window, the city seemed washed clean, every surface carrying a faint silver sheen. Children hurried along the sidewalk beneath umbrellas while distant trains disappeared into the mist. It was the sort of weather that made unfinished thoughts feel heavier, lingering just long enough to demand attention.

Somewhere beyond the noise of the city, another story waited. Not a complete story, not even a good one yet, but a collection of scenes, fragments, and questions searching for their shape. The first draft rarely knew where it was going. Its purpose was simply to exist long enough for the writer to discover what came next.
