starting over, again

For years, I have wanted to go back to college. After getting accepted into Temple University, I thought it was meant to be. Why would I get lucky the second time around? Who knew that going back to school would become a distant thought again? I have been living in Japan for three years and still haven’t learned the language, primarily because of stress, overwork, and simply not having the time or energy to truly focus on Japanese. That’s why I thought going back to college was perfect. I would finally have the opportunity to learn Japanese, expand my options in Japan, and pursue the career I have always wanted in the tech industry.

Now, all of that has come to a standstill because I simply can’t afford college. I have applied to various private loan lenders, and every one of them requires me to have a co-signer. There is no realistic way for me to pay tuition out of pocket on my salary in Japan. I had so many plans for going back to school. I was excited about finally moving forward, changing careers, learning Japanese, and building a life here that felt more sustainable.

And now… I’m back at square one. Do I finally give up?
